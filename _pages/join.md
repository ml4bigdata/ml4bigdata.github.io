# Join us

We work on the mathematical foundations and practical methods of **trustworthy
artificial intelligence** — federated learning, explainable AI, and fairness in
machine learning — at the Department of Computer Science, Aalto University.

## Who we are looking for

**Doctoral researchers.** A strong background in mathematics is what matters
most: probability, linear algebra, optimisation. Prior publications are not
expected. You should be comfortable reading a proof and writing code, and
interested in why a method works rather than only that it does.

**Post-doctoral researchers.** A completed PhD in machine learning, statistics,
signal processing, optimisation, or a closely related field, and a track record
that overlaps with [our research](/research/).

**Thesis students at Aalto.** Bachelor's and master's thesis topics are
available in all of our research areas. Aalto students are welcome to get in
touch directly.

## What we work on

- Federated learning for network-structured and heterogeneous data
- Explainable and intrinsically simulatable models
- Fairness, accountability and transparency in AI
- Machine learning for healthcare

Our work is supported by the Research Council of Finland (MATHFUL), the Jane and
Aatos Erkko Foundation (TRUST-FELT), and Business Finland (FLAIG). Details of
each grant are on the [research page](/research/).

## How to apply

Write to [alex.jung@aalto.fi](mailto:alex.jung@aalto.fi) with:

1. A short statement of what you want to work on, and why this group
2. Your CV, including a list of relevant courses and grades
3. A writing sample — a thesis, a course project report, or a preprint
4. Names of one or two referees

Please put **"PhD application"** or **"Post-doc application"** in the subject
line. We read everything, and reply to applications that fit the group's
current work.

## Before you write

Two things make an application much easier to evaluate: name a specific paper
or research direction of ours that you want to build on, and say what you would
want to do next with it. Generic applications sent to many groups at once are
easy to recognise and rarely go anywhere.

If you are not sure whether your background fits, write anyway and say what you
are unsure about.
