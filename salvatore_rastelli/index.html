Salvatore Rastelli

📖 Bio

Salvatore Rastelli holds a bachelor’s degree in Physics, which gave him a strong analytical foundation, and later completed a master’s degree in Data Science, focusing on machine learning, statistical modeling, and computational methods. Recently, he developed a strong interest in federated learning and reinforcement learning. His current research focuses on developing a mathematical framework for Federated Reinforcement Learning, aiming to better understand its theoretical foundations and limitations.
