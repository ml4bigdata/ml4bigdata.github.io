Mikko Seesto

📖 Bio

Mikko Seesto is one of the doctoral researchers in the team. His background is in mathematics and optimisation, culminating in a master’s degree in applied mathematics from Aalto University. This background supports him in his research focusing on the theoretical aspects of federated learning. His current efforts are aimed at finding the optimal way of utilising untrustworthy data alongside trustworthy one.
